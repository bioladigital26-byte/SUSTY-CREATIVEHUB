What We Do

SUSTY CREATIVE HUB is a sustainability-driven creative organization dedicated to promoting environmental awareness, youth empowerment, and community transformation through art, education, advocacy, and innovation.

Art & Innovation

Every artwork showcased is made entirely from discarded materials, demonstrating that value exists in what society often throws away. We inspire communities to see waste differently.

Education & Advocacy

Through interactive learning sessions, workshops, and creative demonstrations, we empower young people and stakeholders to embrace innovative approaches to environmental stewardship.

Community Impact

Our initiatives promote responsible consumption, recycling, and climate awareness, directly contributing to the achievement of multiple UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Recycle Art Tour 2.0

In commemoration of World Environment Day 2026, we successfully executed a four-day environmental awareness campaign spanning four major divisions of Lagos State.

Badagry | Epe | Lagos Island | Ikeja (June 1st - 5th, 2026)

The Initiative

The Recycle Art Tour was created to bridge the gap between environmental awareness and practical action by using art as a tool for education, advocacy, and social change. Over the four-day engagement, the initiative brought together students, educators, environmental advocates, government representatives, and community stakeholders.

Participants were exposed to conversations around waste management, responsible consumption, recycling, climate action, and the role individuals can play in protecting the environment.

Official Partnerships

Recycle Art Tour 2.0 was implemented in official partnership with organizations committed to advancing environmental sustainability and climate action across Lagos State:

  • Lagos State Environmental Protection Agency (LASEPA)
  • FABE International Foundation
  • Lagos State Ministry of Environment and Water Resources

Meet the Founder

Progress Jesutomiwa Giwa

Progress Jesutomiwa Giwa is a Nigerian multidisciplinary artist, environmental advocate, and founder of SUSTY CREATIVE HUB. Through his artworks, community projects, and sustainability initiatives, he uses creativity as a tool to address environmental challenges, promote climate awareness, and inspire positive social impact.

Under his leadership, SUSTY CREATIVE HUB has become a beacon of hope, proving that creativity can be a powerful catalyst for environmental action.
