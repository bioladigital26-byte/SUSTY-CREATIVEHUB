Meet the Founder

Progress Jesutomiwa Giwa

Progress Jesutomiwa Giwa is a Nigerian multidisciplinary artist, environmental advocate, and founder of SUSTY CREATIVE HUB. Through his artworks, community projects, and sustainability initiatives, he uses creativity as a tool to address environmental challenges, promote climate awareness, and inspire positive social impact.

Under his leadership, SUSTY CREATIVE HUB has become a beacon of hope, proving that creativity can be a powerful catalyst for environmental action.
